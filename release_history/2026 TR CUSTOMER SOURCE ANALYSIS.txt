  formulas.

  company abbreviations, full company names, aliases, and contact-style values.

FORMULA DEPENDENCIES
- The MARK-UP pricing formulas do not reference columns J or K.
- Customer/company and buyer/contact therefore do not calculate the selling
  price, VAT, landed cost, duty, markup, or quotation totals.
- ACTUAL column J copies MARK-UP column J with a VLOOKUP.
- ACTUAL column K copies MARK-UP column K with a VLOOKUP.
- Therefore these fields matter for downstream identification/reporting, but
  not for customer-facing price calculation.

SAFE AUTOCOMPLETE DESIGN
1. Company remains editable so a new customer can still be entered.
2. Typing a partial value such as GNP presents matching companies such as GNPD
   and GNPK.
3. After an exact company match, buyer suggestions are filtered to contacts
   historically associated with that company.
4. A single known contact can be filled automatically.
5. When several contacts exist, the employee must choose the correct buyer for
   that RFQ. The app does not guess based only on frequency.
6. The selected raw values continue to map to Tracker columns J and K without
   changing Macrotech formulas or formatting logic.

FIELDS NOT YET SAFE TO AUTOFILL
- Official legal company name
- Billing or delivery address
- TIN
- Phone number
- Buyer title/department

These should be added only from an approved authoritative customer master, not
inferred from historical quotation rows.
